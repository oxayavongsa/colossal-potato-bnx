Sacramento meal prep, delivered

Colossal Potato makes high-protein loaded potato bowls — chicken bacon ranch, steakhouse, BBQ chicken, pulled pork, breakfast bowls and a vegetarian sweet potato — cooked fresh and delivered same day across Sacramento, West Sacramento, Natomas, Elk Grove, Rancho Cordova, Carmichael, Citrus Heights and Arden-Arcade. Every order is a 5-pack: a whole week of lunches, no shopping, no cooking.

Do you deliver in my area?

We deliver across Sacramento and nearby areas like West Sacramento, Natomas, Elk Grove, Rancho Cordova, Carmichael, Citrus Heights and Arden-Arcade — within 20 driving miles. Enter your address at checkout and we'll confirm it instantly. Delivery is $7 up to 10 miles or $14 up to 20 miles, one fee per order.

How does ordering work?

Pick a bowl and get a 5-pack of the same bowl, preordered and prepaid online. Order by 7 PM for 9–11 AM delivery the next morning, by 11 AM for 2–4 PM delivery, or by 3 PM for 7–9 PM delivery. You can book up to 3 months ahead.

How long do the bowls last?

Keep them in the fridge and enjoy within 4 days, or freeze within 2 days for up to 2 months. Reheat in 2–3 minutes. Freezing? Choose sauce on the side.

Are the bowls high protein?

Yes — our chicken, steak, pork and breakfast bowls pack about 47–75g of protein each. Full calories, carbs, fat and allergens for every bowl are on our nutrition page. See nutrition facts →

Do you have low-carb or breakfast options?

Yes. Any bowl can swap the potato for cauliflower mash for $5 per pack, and we have two breakfast bowls: Country Breakfast and Breakfast Supreme.
